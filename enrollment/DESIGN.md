# IITI enrollment interface

This is a refinement of the existing enrollment-office interface. Preserve the school logo, forest-green identity, familiar navigation, and task terminology.

- Brand: #2E522A; dark navigation: #173c2c; supporting green: #1f5a3c.
- Canvas: #f4f7f5. White panels, subtle borders, muted green table headings.
- Use green for selection and primary actions; amber/red retain their status meanings.
- System sans-serif type. Fixed heading sizes, compact labels, tabular numerals for data.
- Panels use 12px radii. Controls use 6–8px radii where practical. Shadows belong to overlays.
- No gradients, decorative background shapes, glass effects, or hover-lift effects.
- One page title. Keep actions near their related data. Prefer compact rows to decorative cards.
- The sidebar becomes a drawer below 1024px. Tables scroll inside their container. Phone inputs use at least 16px text.
- Preserve keyboard focus, visible labels, selected/disabled states, loading feedback, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Icons are the locally bundled Font Awesome 6.4 family in public/vendor/fontawesome.
